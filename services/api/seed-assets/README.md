# Development catalogue photos

These photos are used only by the explicit development seed, never hardcoded in
the Android client. Store owners replace them with their own product photography.

- `tomatoes.jpg`: National Cancer Institute, 1994, public domain US government
  work. [Source and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tomato_(1).jpg).
- `bananas.jpg`: Paolo Neo, released into the public domain.
  [Source and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Banana_on_white_background.jpg).

Original JPEGs retained without modification. Seed copies them to local media.

The following neutral, unbranded catalogue photographs were generated for this
project with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 24 September 2026:

- `potatoes.jpg`, `onions.jpg`, `fresh-milk.jpg`, `sona-masoori-rice.jpg`,
  `whole-wheat-atta.jpg`, `toor-dal.jpg`, `sunflower-oil.jpg`, `spices.jpg` and
  `tea.jpg`.

The shared prompt direction requested a centred, realistic grocery catalogue
photograph on a warm off-white studio background with soft daylight, natural
shadows, plain packaging where needed, and no labels, logos, text, watermarks or
people. These are generic visual aids: they intentionally do not reproduce the
commercial packaging of the branded seed products. Generated PNGs were resized
to 1024 px and saved as quality-82 JPEGs for the development catalogue.
